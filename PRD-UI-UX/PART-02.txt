FINAL QUALITY REQUIREMENT 
The application should look like a $50M ARR SaaS platform, not a hackathon project. Every page should have 
consistent spacing, reusable components, polished charts, realistic mock data, thoughtful empty/loading/error states, 
and enterprise-grade navigation. The UI should feel like a product that could be presented to executives and 
investors. 
 
One Improvement Before Part 3 
After reviewing the PDF again, I think we can make the product even stronger by renaming and restructuring some 
navigation. 
Instead of a traditional sidebar: 
●​
Dashboard 
●​
Clients 
●​
Reports 
●​
Tasks 
I recommend organizing it around business outcomes, for example: 
●​
Command Center 
●​
Accounts 
●​
Health Intelligence 
●​
Growth Intelligence 
●​
Work Queue 
●​
Insights 
●​
Administration 
This subtle change makes the product feel more like an AI operations platform than a CRM. It also aligns much better 
with the vision described in the PDF and gives the application a distinctive identity during your demo.
